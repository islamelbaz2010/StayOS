# CLOSED ALPHA EXECUTION PLAN — StayOS

**Prepared by:** Executive Marketplace Operations Board  
**Date:** 2026-08-03  
**Purpose:** Define the complete operational plan to launch the StayOS Closed Alpha.

---

## 1. Closed Alpha Definition

The Closed Alpha is a 4-week, invitation-only launch in one or two adjacent Cairo neighborhoods. It will use real hosts, real properties, and real bookings. The goal is not scale. The goal is to prove the marketplace loop: supply → demand → booking → trust → repeat.

**Evidence from the repository:**
- `knowledge/marketplace/cold_start_playbook.md` — first 10 transactions, manual operations
- `knowledge/marketplace/marketplace_lifecycle.md` — Stage 1 ignition rules
- `knowledge/founder/scaling_playbook.md` — Cairo Zone 1 playbook
- `MARKETPLACE_SUPPLY_STRATEGY.md` — cold start and scaling milestones
- `SPRINT3_RECOMMENDATIONS.md` — Sprint 3 re-scope to closed alpha preparation

---

## 2. Geographic Scope

**Primary area:** New Cairo + Rehab (or Maadi + Degla as fallback).

**Why:**
- High concentration of GCC expats and family travelers.
- Strong property management infrastructure.
- Close to Cairo International Airport.
- Higher average nightly rate.

**Rule:** No listings outside the target area will be published during Closed Alpha. Non-target leads are waitlisted.

## 3. Week-by-Week Plan

### 3.1 Week 1: Supply Lock-In

**Objective:** Sign the first institutional supply partners and seed 20+ listings.

**Required team:**
- Founder (50% time)
- 1 Supply Manager
- 1 Onboarding Specialist
- 1 Field Photographer/Inspector

**Required listings:**
- 20 live or draft listings by end of week.
- Minimum 5 from institutional partners (property managers, serviced apartments, small hotels).

**Required hosts:**
- 3 institutional partners signed.
- 5 individual hosts in pipeline.

**Activities:**

| Day | Activity | Owner |
|-----|----------|-------|
| 1 | Confirm target area, refresh seed inventory list | Supply Director |
| 1–2 | Founder calls top 10 institutional prospects | Founder |
| 1–3 | Supply Manager reaches out to 20 property managers | Supply Manager |
| 2–4 | Collect and import first property data | Onboarding Specialist |
| 3–5 | Schedule and complete first 5 property inspections | Field Staff |
| 4–5 | Upload photos, set pricing, submit for verification | Onboarding Specialist |
| 5 | Review Week 1 supply targets | Supply Director + Founder |

**Week 1 exit criteria:**
- [ ] 20 listings created (draft, pending, or live).
- [ ] 3 institutional partners signed or in pilot agreement.
- [ ] KYC and verification workflow tested with at least 3 real hosts.

### 3.2 Week 2: Listing Publication

**Objective:** Publish 50 verified, bookable listings.

**Required team:**
- Founder (30% time)
- 2 Supply Managers
- 2 Onboarding Specialists
- 2 Field Staff
- 1 KYC Reviewer
- 1 Listing Verifier

**Required listings:**
- 50 verified and published.
- Active listing rate ≥ 80%.

**Required hosts:**
- 10+ active hosts.
- All hosts with completed KYC.

**Activities:**

| Day | Activity | Owner |
|-----|----------|-------|
| 1 | Review and approve all Week 1 listings | Trust & Safety |
| 1–3 | Complete 10+ additional inspections | Field Staff |
| 2–4 | Resolve KYC and document gaps | KYC Reviewer |
| 3–5 | Publish approved listings, notify hosts | Onboarding Specialist |
| 4–5 | Begin host success calls for published listings | Host Success |
| 5 | Review Week 2 targets and quality | Operations Manager |

**Week 2 exit criteria:**
- [ ] 50 listings live.
- [ ] ≥ 80% active listing rate.
- [ ] All live listings have KYC-approved hosts.
- [ ] Host success follow-up calls scheduled.

### 3.3 Week 3: First Bookings

**Objective:** Generate the first 10 manual or platform bookings.

**Required team:**
- Founder (30% time)
- 1 Operations Manager
- 1 Operations Agent
- 1 Host Success Manager
- 1 Support Lead

**Required bookings:**
- 5 confirmed bookings.
- 1 completed stay.

**Activities:**

| Day | Activity | Owner |
|-----|----------|-------|
| 1 | Identify first potential guests (founder network, corporate contacts) | Founder |
| 1–3 | Manually match guests to listings via WhatsApp | Operations Manager |
| 2–4 | Coordinate check-ins, verify guest-ready properties | Operations Agent |
| 3–5 | Process first payments through Paymob/Stripe or manual escrow | Finance Lead |
| 4–5 | Execute first turnover and check-in | Operations Agent |
| 5 | Collect first guest review | Host Success Manager |

**Week 3 exit criteria:**
- [ ] 5 confirmed bookings.
- [ ] 1 completed stay with review.
- [ ] Payment and escrow process validated.
- [ ] Operations clock executed at least once.

### 3.4 Week 4: Learn and Refine

**Objective:** Reach 10 transactions, collect feedback, and decide Go / No-Go for continued expansion.

**Required team:**
- All of Week 3 team
- Product Lead
- Founder

**Required bookings:**
- 10 cumulative transactions (confirmed or completed).
- 5 completed stays.
- 3 verified guest reviews.

**Activities:**

| Day | Activity | Owner |
|-----|----------|-------|
| 1 | Review Week 3 bookings, identify friction | Operations Manager |
| 1–3 | Drive 5 more bookings (manual + platform) | Founder + Operations |
| 2–4 | Conduct post-stay calls with hosts and guests | Host Success |
| 3–4 | Compile list of product/operational fixes | Product Lead |
| 4–5 | Run Go / No-Go review | Founder + Leadership |
| 5 | Document Closed Alpha learnings and next steps | Operations Manager |

**Week 4 exit criteria:**
- [ ] 10 cumulative transactions.
- [ ] 5 completed stays.
- [ ] 3 verified guest reviews.
- [ ] Go / No-Go decision documented.

## 8. Go / No-Go Criteria

### 8.1 GO Criteria

The Closed Alpha is a GO if **all** of the following are met by Week 4:

1. **Supply:** 50+ verified, live listings in target area.
2. **Activation:** ≥ 80% active listing rate.
3. **Transactions:** 10 cumulative transactions.
4. **Completion:** 5 completed stays.
5. **Trust:** 3+ verified guest reviews with average ≥ 4.0.
6. **Safety:** 0 P0 safety incidents.
7. **Host confidence:** Host NPS ≥ 50.
8. **Operations:** All bookings supported without platform failure.
9. **Team:** Operations playbook documented and trainable.
10. **Founder decision:** Founder approves continuation.

### 8.2 NO-GO Criteria

The Closed Alpha is a NO-GO if **any** of the following occur:

1. **Supply shortfall:** Fewer than 30 live listings by Week 4.
2. **Active listing rate:** < 60%.
3. **Booking shortfall:** Fewer than 5 transactions.
4. **Trust failure:** A serious safety incident, fraud, or unresolved dispute.
5. **Host churn:** More than 30% of published hosts deactivate or block calendars.
6. **Guest dissatisfaction:** Average review < 3.5.
7. **Payment failure:** Payments cannot be processed or payouts cannot be released.
8. **Founder decision:** Founder decides the signal is not strong enough.

### 8.3 Conditional GO

If the team hits supply and booking targets but has operational issues, the Closed Alpha may continue with a Week 5 refinement sprint before expansion.

## 11. Risks

| Risk | Likelihood | Impact | Mitigation |
|------|------------|--------|------------|
| Supply target missed | Medium | High | Founder-led BD, zero-commission pilot, manual import. |
| Payment integration not live | Medium | High | Use manual escrow (InstaPay/bank transfer) for first 10. |
| Quality failure | Medium | High | Three-gate verification, reject substandard listings. |
| Host churn | Medium | High | Fast first booking, pricing coaching, proactive host success. |
| Guest no-show or cancellation | Low | Medium | Confirm bookings 24h in advance. |
| Field staff bottleneck | Medium | Medium | Hire 2 photographers/inspectors in target area. |
| Platform bugs | Medium | Medium | Use manual processes as fallback. |

## 12. Post-Alpha Decision

If GO:
- Begin Stage 2 (Liquidity) in the same area.
- Add supply to reach 100 listings.
- Open limited guest marketing.
- Expand team to include dedicated support and finance.

If NO-GO:
- Halt expansion.
- Diagnose the binding constraint (supply, demand, trust, operations).
- Run a 2–4 week recovery sprint.
- Re-test before any public activity.
