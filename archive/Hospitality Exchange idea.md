1. CONCEPT
StayOS is exploring a focused feature called:

Reciprocal Hosting Match
The concept is extremely simple:

Match exactly two travelers who are traveling to each other's home countries during the same travel dates, so each traveler hosts the other for free at the same time.

Example
Traveler A

Home country: Egypt 🇪🇬

Destination: France 🇫🇷

Travel dates: September 10–20

Traveler B

Home country: France 🇫🇷

Destination: Egypt 🇪🇬

Travel dates: September 10–20

StayOS matches them.

During the same period:

Traveler B stays with Traveler A in Egypt — FREE

Traveler A stays with Traveler B in France — FREE

The accommodation exchange is strictly 1-to-1 and simultaneous.

2. NON-NEGOTIABLE PRODUCT RULES
These rules define the concept.

Do NOT change them during analysis.

Required Match Conditions
A match is valid only when:

There are exactly 2 travelers.

Traveler A lives in Country A.

Traveler B lives in Country B.

Traveler A wants to travel to Country B.

Traveler B wants to travel to Country A.

Both travelers travel during the same dates.

Traveler A can host Traveler B.

Traveler B can host Traveler A.

Both agree to the exchange.

Accommodation is free for both travelers.
