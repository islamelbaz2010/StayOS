# Business Model

## Purpose
Define how StayOS creates, delivers, and captures value as an AI-powered accommodation marketplace focused on the MENA region.

## Scope
Applies to all marketplace participants (hosts, guests, platform), all property types, and all MENA launch markets. Does not include detailed engineering implementation.

## Owner
Founder / COO

## Inputs
- Market research on MENA travel behavior
- Regulatory requirements per operating country
- Competitive pricing benchmarks
- Host acquisition cost estimates
- Guest acquisition funnel data

## Outputs
- Commercial strategy
- Pricing and commission framework
- Host value proposition
- Guest value proposition
- Go-to-market thesis

## Workflow
1. Hosts list verified inventory on the platform.
2. Guests search, compare, and book stays using localized payment methods.
3. StayOS collects payment, holds funds in escrow, and releases to the host after check-in or a defined hold period.
4. The platform earns commission and service fees on each successful booking.
5. AI tools improve matching, pricing recommendations, and trust verification.

## Exceptions
- B2B or corporate contracts may use custom commission rates approved by the Founder.
- Long-term stays (>30 nights) may use a separate monthly billing model.
- Charitable or government partnerships may override standard fees with written approval.

## KPIs
- Gross Booking Value (GBV) per month
- Take rate (platform revenue / GBV)
- Host listing conversion rate
- Guest repeat booking rate
- Customer Acquisition Cost (CAC) for hosts and guests

## Dependencies
- Marketplace liquidity (sufficient listings and demand)
- Payment and escrow infrastructure
- Identity and KYC verification
- Local legal entity and tax registration

## Best Practices
- Lead with host earnings transparency; show expected payout before publishing.
- Localize pricing, currency, and cancellation messaging for each MENA market.
- Prioritize trust signals (reviews, verified badges, response rate) in search ranking.

## Review Frequency
Quarterly, or after any major market expansion.
