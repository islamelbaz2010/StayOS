# Guest Operations Playbook

## Purpose
Define the complete guest experience, support journeys, and operational workflows from first visit through booking, stay, cancellation, refund, complaint, and repeat visit.

## Scope
Applies to all guest-facing operational activities and support workflows. Excludes product development of the guest application.

## Owner
Customer Success Director / Guest Support Lead

## Inputs
- Business_Model.md
- Marketplace_Model.md
- Trust_Model.md
- Escrow_Model.md
- Booking, payment, and review data
- Support ticket and complaint data
- Refund and cancellation policies

## Outputs
- Guest journey maps and SOPs
- Support and complaint resolution outcomes
- Refund and cancellation records
- Guest satisfaction and retention metrics

## Workflow

### 1. Guest Journey
1. Discovery: guest searches, filters, and compares listings.
2. Booking: guest completes identity verification, selects dates, and pays.
3. Pre-arrival: guest receives confirmation, check-in instructions, and reminders.
4. Stay: guest checks in, communicates with host, and uses the accommodation.
5. Post-stay: guest checks out, reviews, and may contact support for issues.
6. Retention: guest receives personalized re-engagement and loyalty incentives.

### 2. Booking Journey SOP
1. Confirm guest identity is verified and payment is authorized.
2. Send booking confirmation with property details, host contact, and cancellation policy.
3. Calendar is locked for the booked dates.
4. Trigger pre-arrival reminder 24 hours before check-in.
5. Confirm check-in completion and update booking status.
6. Release escrow to host per Escrow_Model.md.

### 3. Cancellation Journey SOP
1. Guest or host initiates cancellation through the platform.
2. Apply cancellation policy to determine refund amount.
3. If eligible, process refund to the original payment method or wallet.
4. Release or recover host payout per Escrow_Model.md.
5. Communicate decision and reason clearly to both parties.
6. Log cancellation reason for trend analysis.

### 4. Support Journey SOP
1. Guest contacts support through chat, email, or phone.
2. Ticket is categorized and assigned a priority (P1–P4).
3. Tier-1 resolves standard issues using approved scripts and policies.
4. Tier-2 handles refunds, disputes, and policy exceptions.
5. Escalate per Escalation_Matrix.md for safety, fraud, or high-value disputes.
6. Close ticket, request feedback, and update knowledge base.

### 5. Refund Journey SOP
1. Support validates refund eligibility per cancellation policy or exception decision.
2. Finance Lead or authorized role approves exceptions per Approval_Matrix.md.
3. Refund is processed through the original payment method or wallet.
4. Escrow ledger is adjusted and reconciliation is flagged.
5. Guest and host are notified with transaction reference.
6. Dispute or chargeback refunds follow the Dispute Resolution Flow.

### 6. Complaint Journey SOP
1. Capture guest complaint, evidence (photos, messages, receipts), and desired outcome.
2. Acknowledge receipt within SLA (P3 < 4 hours, P2 < 1 hour, P1 < 15 minutes).
3. Attempt host-mediated resolution when appropriate.
4. If unresolved or high severity, escalate to Trust & Safety or Operations Director.
5. Decide outcome and compensation within policy limits.
6. Follow up with guest, update satisfaction score, and close loop.

### 7. Guest Satisfaction Flow
1. Trigger post-stay review request 24 hours after checkout.
2. Monitor review sentiment and low scores.
3. Reach out to guests with score <= 3 to understand issues and recover relationship.
4. Share positive feedback with hosts.
5. Aggregate insights for product and operations improvements.

### 8. Guest Success Playbook
- Help guests find the right listing quickly with localized search and filters.
- Proactively communicate check-in, cancellation, and refund policies before payment.
- Resolve issues with empathy and clear ownership.
- Protect guest funds through escrow and transparent refund rules.
- Turn complaints into retention opportunities.

## Exceptions
- Force majeure (weather, political events, pandemic) may trigger special cancellation/refund policies approved by the COO.
- VIP or B2B guests may receive expedited support with documented approval.
- Guests who abuse refunds or file fraudulent complaints may be restricted per Trust_Model.md.

## KPIs
- Guest NPS
- First response time (FRT) and resolution time
- Refund processing time
- Cancellation and refund rates
- Repeat booking rate
- Complaint escalation rate
- Post-stay review completion rate
- Average review rating

## Dependencies
- Business_Model.md
- Trust_Model.md
- Escrow_Model.md
- Approval_Matrix.md
- Escalation_Matrix.md
- Support ticketing and communication tools
- Payment and refund systems

## Best Practices
- Respond to guests faster than they expect; speed builds trust.
- Be transparent about timelines and ownership of each issue.
- Empathize first, policy second, except for safety or fraud.
- Use complaints to identify systemic issues in onboarding, listings, or policies.

## Review Frequency
Monthly; cancellation/refund policy reviewed quarterly.
