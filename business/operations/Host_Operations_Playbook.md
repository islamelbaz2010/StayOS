# Host Operations Playbook

## Purpose
Define the complete lifecycle, processes, and success framework for hosts on StayOS, from first contact through suspension or offboarding.

## Scope
Applies to all hosts and host-facing operational activities before and after launch. Excludes product development of host tools.

## Owner
Host Success Lead / Operations Director

## Inputs
- Marketplace_Model.md
- Trust_Model.md
- Escrow_Model.md
- KYC/verification results
- Listing data and quality scores
- Host performance metrics
- Support and incident reports

## Outputs
- Onboarded, activated, and quality-monitored host base
- Host performance reports
- Suspension and termination records
- Host feedback and improvement plans

## Workflow

### 1. Host Journey
1. Lead generation via partnerships, digital channels, or sales outreach.
2. Host signs up and verifies identity.
3. Host creates and publishes a reviewed listing.
4. Host receives first booking, manages calendar, and welcomes guest.
5. Guest checks out; payout is released after the hold period.
6. Host accumulates reviews and a quality score.
7. High-performing hosts receive priority placement and support; low performers enter improvement or suspension flow.

### 2. Host Lifecycle Stages
| Stage | Criteria | Action |
|---|---|---|
| Lead | Expressed interest | Nurture with content, demo, onboarding invite |
| Registered | Account created | Send verification checklist |
| Verified | KYC passed | Enable listing creation |
| Listed | Published listing approved | Begin search exposure |
| Active | Received first booking | Provide onboarding call and support |
| Mature | >5 completed stays, quality score > 75 | Priority ranking, loyalty perks |
| At Risk | Quality score < 50 or SLA misses | Improvement plan and coaching |
| Suspended | Policy violation or no improvement | Suspend listings, notify, document |
| Offboarded | Account closed or terminated | Final reconciliation and data retention |

### 3. Host Onboarding SOP
1. Verify host identity through KYC (government ID + selfie).
2. Collect tax/payout information (bank account or wallet).
3. Provide localized host welcome kit and training links.
4. Guide host to create the first listing.
5. Schedule or automate a 15-minute onboarding call within 48 hours.
6. Confirm host understands cancellation, refund, and escrow policies.

### 4. Host Verification SOP
1. Confirm KYC document validity and biometric match.
2. Cross-check government ID against sanctions/watchlists.
3. Verify payout account ownership matches the verified legal name.
4. Flag and escalate mismatches to Trust & Safety Lead.
5. Mark host as verified or request additional documents.

### 5. Host Training SOP
1. Deliver product walkthrough (calendar, pricing, messaging, check-in).
2. Provide hospitality standards (response time, cleanliness, amenities, guest communication).
3. Explain payout timing, refund rules, and dispute handling.
4. Share localized tips for MENA guests (privacy, cultural expectations, payment methods).
5. Quiz or checklist completion recorded in host profile.

### 6. Host Activation SOP
1. Confirm listing is published and meets quality score threshold.
2. Review pricing and calendar availability.
3. Enable search exposure and, if eligible, featured placement.
4. Send personalized first-booking tips.
5. Track activation KPIs and follow up with inactive hosts after 7 days.

### 7. Host Suspension SOP
1. Document reason (policy violation, fraud, quality, or safety).
2. Consult Trust & Safety Lead or Operations Director per Approval_Matrix.md.
3. Suspend active listings and block new bookings.
4. Notify host with clear reason, evidence, and appeal instructions.
5. Hold pending payouts until investigation is complete.
6. Resolve within 5 business days; reinstate, warn, or terminate.

### 8. Host Quality Score
Weighted composite updated weekly:
- Verified identity and documents (20%)
- Listing accuracy and photo quality (20%)
- Response rate and response time (15%)
- Acceptance and cancellation rate (15%)
- Review rating and review count (20%)
- Policy compliance and incident history (10%)

### 9. Host Performance KPIs
- Host activation rate (% verified hosts with published listing)
- Time to first booking
- Occupancy rate by host
- Average review rating
- Cancellation rate
- Response rate and time
- Payout on-time rate
- Host NPS

## Exceptions
- Launch partners may be fast-tracked through verification with documented approval.
- Hosts without local ID may be verified using passports or residence permits per local law.
- Repeated policy violations bypass improvement plans and move directly to suspension.

## Dependencies
- Trust_Model.md
- Marketplace_Model.md
- Escrow_Model.md
- Approval_Matrix.md
- Escalation_Matrix.md
- KYC and listing review tools

## Best Practices
- Treat host onboarding as a service, not a checklist; proactive follow-up improves activation.
- Make quality score transparent to hosts and publish improvement tips.
- Resolve suspensions quickly to preserve supply liquidity.
- Maintain a recorded escalation trail for every suspension or termination.

## Review Frequency
Monthly for playbook content; host quality score updated weekly.
