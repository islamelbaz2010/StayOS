# Launch Readiness

## Purpose
Provide a single readiness checklist and gate review for StayOS before onboarding the first real host or guest.

## Scope
Covers business, operational, host, guest, marketplace, finance, support, trust, and dashboard readiness. Technical launch readiness is outside scope and tracked separately.

## Owner
COO / Operations Director

## Outputs
- Launch readiness score
- Go / No-Go recommendation
- Remaining gaps and remediation owners
- Launch checklist sign-off

## Workflow
1. Review each readiness area below.
2. Mark status as Not Started, In Progress, Ready, or N/A.
3. Identify blockers and assign owner.
4. COO presents readiness to Founder for go/no-go decision.
5. After launch, transition this document into a post-launch operational review template.

## Launch Readiness Checklist

### 1. Business Model Readiness
- [ ] Commission and service fee structure finalized and approved by Founder.
- [ ] Cancellation and refund policies published in Arabic and English.
- [ ] Dispute resolution flow documented and communicated.
- [ ] Marketplace rules and trust model approved.
- [ ] Escrow and payout timing confirmed with Finance.

### 2. Revenue & Finance Readiness
- [ ] Pricing engine and fee formulas validated against product implementation.
- [ ] Escrow accounting process and reconciliation owner assigned.
- [ ] Refund and chargeback allocation rules approved.
- [ ] Payment provider contracts and settlement timelines confirmed.
- [ ] Financial forecast and launch budget approved.

### 3. Operational Model Readiness
- [ ] Daily, weekly, and monthly operational cadence defined.
- [ ] Roles and responsibilities assigned and backups documented.
- [ ] Approval matrix published and understood by all leads.
- [ ] Escalation matrix published and tested with contact list.
- [ ] Communication and decision-making workflows documented.

### 4. Host Operations Readiness
- [ ] Host onboarding, verification, training, and activation SOPs finalized.
- [ ] Host suspension and appeal process documented.
- [ ] Host Quality Score formula and thresholds approved.
- [ ] Initial batch of target hosts identified and outreach planned.
- [ ] Host support channel and first-response SLA defined.

### 5. Guest Operations Readiness
- [ ] Guest journey, booking, cancellation, support, refund, and complaint journeys mapped.
- [ ] Guest satisfaction flow and NPS process defined.
- [ ] Support scripts and macros prepared in Arabic and English.
- [ ] Refund and complaint decision authority documented.
- [ ] Post-stay review and recovery process defined.

### 6. Marketplace Operations Readiness
- [ ] Listing review, pricing review, and manual verification SOPs ready.
- [ ] Fraud detection rules and manual review workflow documented.
- [ ] Incident handling and emergency escalation SOPs tested.
- [ ] Booking and payment monitoring dashboards ready.
- [ ] Escrow release and chargeback handling SOPs approved.

### 7. Trust & Safety Readiness
- [ ] KYC/ID verification process operational and monitored.
- [ ] Trust signals, badges, and scoring published.
- [ ] Fraud and dispute playbooks reviewed by Trust & Safety Lead.
- [ ] Data handling and privacy commitments documented.
- [ ] Emergency contact and legal escalation path confirmed.

### 8. KPI & Dashboard Readiness
- [ ] KPI definitions, formulas, targets, owners, and frequencies documented.
- [ ] Founder Dashboard wireframes or reports created.
- [ ] Operations Dashboards (daily, weekly, monthly, incident, support, finance) created.
- [ ] Data sources and refresh frequency confirmed.
- [ ] Action thresholds and escalation rules defined.

### 9. Risk & Legal Readiness
- [ ] Risk register created and reviewed with Founder.
- [ ] Top 5 risks have active mitigation owners and deadlines.
- [ ] Local legal review completed for first launch market.
- [ ] Insurance, liability, and host/guest terms reviewed.
- [ ] Terms of service, cancellation policy, and privacy policy published.

### 10. People & Training Readiness
- [ ] Operations team and contractors hired or contracted.
- [ ] SOP training completed and recorded for all operational roles.
- [ ] Shadow/role-play sessions completed for support and trust scenarios.
- [ ] On-call rotation and escalation contacts tested.
- [ ] Access to tools and dashboards provisioned.

## Readiness Scoring

| Category | Weight | Score (0–10) | Weighted |
|---|---|---|---|
| Business Model | 10% | | |
| Revenue & Finance | 10% | | |
| Operational Model | 10% | | |
| Host Operations | 15% | | |
| Guest Operations | 15% | | |
| Marketplace Operations | 15% | | |
| Trust & Safety | 10% | | |
| KPI & Dashboard | 10% | | |
| Risk & Legal | 5% | | |
| **Total** | **100%** | | |

- Score definitions:
  - 0–3 = critical gaps, not ready
  - 4–6 = significant gaps, launch at risk
  - 7–8 = minor gaps, ready with conditions
  - 9–10 = fully ready

## Exceptions
- Founder may accept risk on a readiness item with documented rationale.
- Soft launch may proceed with a closed beta cohort if critical items are addressed.
- Technical readiness is tracked separately and is not in this checklist.

## KPIs
- Launch readiness score
- Number of open critical gaps
- Days to go/no-go decision
- Closed-beta host and guest sign-up targets

## Dependencies
- All operations documents listed in Inputs
- Technical launch readiness review
- Founder approval
- Budget and headcount availability

## Best Practices
- Score honestly; a false "ready" is more expensive than a delayed launch.
- Assign one owner and one deadline to every open gap.
- Run a tabletop exercise for the top 3 risks before launch.
- Keep this document updated as the first market matures.

## Review Frequency
Before go/no-go; weekly during launch window; monthly after launch until stable.
