# Marketplace Model

## Purpose
Define the rules, incentives, and lifecycle mechanics that keep the StayOS marketplace balanced, liquid, and trustworthy for hosts and guests.

## Scope
Applies to listing quality, search ranking, matching, pricing, availability, booking lifecycle, and marketplace interventions. Excludes product engineering details.

## Owner
COO / Marketplace Operations Manager

## Inputs
- Business_Model.md
- Host and guest behavior data
- Search and conversion analytics
- Listing quality audits
- Fraud and incident reports

## Outputs
- Listing quality standards
- Search ranking policy
- Host/guest matching principles
- Marketplace intervention criteria
- Liquidity targets by city or region

## Workflow
1. Hosts create listings and pass verification and listing review.
2. Listings are ranked by relevance, availability, quality score, response rate, and trust signals.
3. Guests search, filter, and book available units.
4. The platform enforces cancellation, refund, and review rules consistently.
5. Marketplace Operations monitors supply/demand balance, price competitiveness, and quality.
6. Low-quality or high-risk listings are demoted, suspended, or removed based on SOPs.

## Exceptions
- Featured or promoted listings must meet a minimum quality score and are approved by Marketplace Operations.
- New cities may launch with curated inventory before algorithmic ranking is applied.
- Force majeure or safety incidents may require mass rebooking or delisting decisions by the Operations Director.

## KPIs
- Supply liquidity (bookable nights / total nights)
- Demand coverage (bookings / search sessions)
- Average booking lead time
- Listing quality score distribution
- NPS from hosts and guests
- Cancellation and dispute rates

## Dependencies
- Listing and search systems
- Calendar and pricing engine
- Identity, review, and trust infrastructure
- Host and guest operations teams

## Best Practices
- Keep search ranking transparent enough for hosts to improve, opaque enough to prevent gaming.
- Maintain a minimum response-time SLA for hosts to remain bookable.
- Audit a random sample of listings weekly to enforce photo, description, and amenity accuracy.

## Review Frequency
Monthly, with deep review after each market launch.
