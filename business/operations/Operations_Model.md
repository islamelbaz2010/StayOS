# Operations Model

## Purpose
Document the rhythm, structure, and workflows that keep StayOS running day-to-day, week-to-week, and month-to-month before and after launch.

## Scope
Covers operational cadence, department structure, roles, escalation, communication, approvals, and cross-functional workflows. Excludes product development or infrastructure engineering.

## Owner
COO / Operations Director

## Inputs
- Business_Model.md
- Marketplace_Model.md
- Trust_Model.md
- Escrow_Model.md
- Incident and support tickets
- Performance dashboards
- Strategic goals from Founder

## Outputs
- Daily, weekly, and monthly operational plans
- Role definitions and RACI matrices
- Escalation and approval workflows
- Communication cadence
- Decision log

## Workflow
1. Daily Operations
   - Review overnight bookings, cancellations, and support tickets.
   - Monitor escrow releases, chargebacks, and payout failures.
   - Resolve or escalate incidents per the Escalation Matrix.
2. Weekly Operations
   - Host onboarding pipeline review
   - Listing quality audit
   - Support SLA and refund/dispute trends
   - Marketplace liquidity and pricing review
3. Monthly Operations
   - Financial close and reconciliation
   - Host and guest satisfaction review
   - Fraud and trust score review
   - Operational KPI scorecard and OKR review
4. Cross-Functional Coordination
   - Operations syncs with Product, Engineering, Finance, Marketing, and Support via weekly standups.
   - Major decisions are recorded in the decision log.

## Exceptions
- Critical incidents bypass standard cadence and trigger emergency response immediately.
- Founder may call ad-hoc operational reviews for funding, partnerships, or crises.
- New market launches require a separate launch operations plan.

## KPIs
- Daily ticket backlog
- Weekly SLA compliance
- Monthly gross margin
- Operational expense ratio
- Employee/contractor capacity utilization

## Dependencies
- Organization_Structure.md
- Roles_and_Responsibilities.md
- Approval_Matrix.md
- Escalation_Matrix.md
- Ticketing and monitoring tools
- Finance and marketplace dashboards

## Best Practices
- Automate routine monitoring and alerts; reserve human review for exceptions.
- Keep escalation paths short and well known.
- Document every exception and decision in the decision log.
- Review processes quarterly for efficiency and risk.

## Review Frequency
Quarterly, or after any organizational change.
