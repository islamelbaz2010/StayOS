# Organization Structure

## Purpose
Define the pre-launch and early-stage operating structure of StayOS, including departments, reporting lines, and staffing plan for the first market launch.

## Scope
Applies to internal operations roles and responsibilities required before onboarding the first real host or guest. Does not include engineering, product, or executive hiring plans beyond operational scope.

## Owner
Founder / COO

## Inputs
- Business_Model.md
- Operations_Model.md
- Launch readiness timeline
- Budget and headcount constraints
- Local labor and outsourcing options

## Outputs
- Department structure chart
- Role titles and reporting lines
- Headcount plan by phase
- Key external partners and vendors

## Workflow
```
Founder / CEO
└── COO
    ├── Operations Director
    │   ├── Marketplace Operations Manager
    │   ├── Trust & Safety Lead
    │   └── Host Success Lead
    ├── Customer Success Director
    │   ├── Guest Support Lead
    │   └── Community Manager
    ├── Finance Lead
    │   └── Payments & Reconciliation Analyst
    └── Hospitality Consultant (advisor / fractional)
```

## Phase 1 Staffing Plan
- Founder: strategy, fundraising, key partnerships
- COO: daily execution, cross-functional coordination
- Operations Director: process design, SOPs, quality
- Marketplace Operations Manager: listing review, fraud, payments monitoring
- Trust & Safety Lead: KYC/verification, incident handling, disputes
- Host Success Lead: onboarding, activation, performance
- Customer Success Director: guest support, satisfaction, retention
- Guest Support Lead: tier-1 and tier-2 support
- Finance Lead: escrow, payouts, reconciliation, reporting
- Payments & Reconciliation Analyst: daily reconciliation and payout monitoring

## Exceptions
- Contractors may cover Trust & Safety verification, moderation, and multilingual support.
- Hospitality Consultant may be retained part-time during pre-launch and quality calibration.
- Finance Lead may be fractional until transaction volume exceeds 500 bookings per month.

## KPIs
- Time-to-hire for critical roles
- Staff capacity coverage by function
- Training completion rate
- Employee/contractor NPS

## Dependencies
- Budget approval
- Local employment contracts or B2B agreements
- Access to operations tools and dashboards
- Decision authority defined in Approval_Matrix.md

## Best Practices
- Keep layers flat for speed; avoid over-hiring before product/market fit is validated.
- Document SOPs before adding headcount.
- Cross-train support and operations staff on safety, payments, and escalation.

## Review Frequency
Monthly until launch; quarterly after launch.
