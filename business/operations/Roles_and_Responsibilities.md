# Roles and Responsibilities

## Purpose
Define operational roles, key responsibilities, and decision authority for the StayOS launch phase.

## Scope
Applies to all operational roles directly involved in marketplace launch and daily operations. Engineering, product, and marketing roles are referenced only where they interface with operations.

## Owner
COO / Operations Director

## Inputs
- Organization_Structure.md
- Operations_Model.md
- SOPs and playbooks
- Headcount plan

## Outputs
- Role descriptions
- Responsibility matrix
- Decision ownership map
- Hiring and onboarding checklists

## Workflow
1. Identify operational workstreams (Host, Guest, Marketplace, Trust & Safety, Finance, Support).
2. Assign a single owner and backup for each workstream.
3. Define key responsibilities, deliverables, and metrics for each role.
4. Publish and review with the team before launch.
5. Update when roles or responsibilities change.

## Roles

### Founder / CEO
- Sets vision, funding, strategic partnerships, and risk appetite.
- Approves major policy changes, market expansions, and financial commitments.

### COO
- Owns day-to-day operations, cross-functional coordination, and launch readiness.
- Ensures operational KPIs, staffing, and SOPs are in place.

### Operations Director
- Designs, maintains, and audits operational SOPs and playbooks.
- Manages Marketplace Operations, Trust & Safety, and Host Success leads.
- Owns escalation decisions for incidents, fraud, and host suspensions.

### Marketplace Operations Manager
- Reviews new listings and pricing.
- Monitors search ranking, liquidity, and marketplace health.
- Manages fraud detection, manual verifications, and listing quality audits.

### Trust & Safety Lead
- Owns KYC/verification operations, fraud investigation, and incident escalation.
- Reviews high-risk accounts and disputes.
- Maintains the risk register and fraud playbook.

### Host Success Lead
- Drives host onboarding, activation, and quality.
- Monitors host KPIs and runs the Host Success Playbook.
- Escalates host suspensions and terminations.

### Customer Success Director
- Owns guest support, satisfaction, and retention.
- Sets support SLAs, refund policies, and complaint handling workflows.
- Manages the Guest Support Lead and community team.

### Guest Support Lead
- Runs daily support queues and escalations.
- Coaches support agents on tone, policy, and resolution quality.
- Reports on SLA, satisfaction, and top issues.

### Finance Lead
- Owns escrow, payouts, reconciliation, revenue recognition, and reporting.
- Approves refunds, chargebacks, and financial exceptions.
- Produces weekly financial dashboard for Founder.

### Payments & Reconciliation Analyst
- Performs daily reconciliation of escrow and provider settlements.
- Monitors payout failures and refund transactions.
- Flags discrepancies within 24 hours.

## Exceptions
- Contractors and part-time staff may own tactical tasks under the supervision of a full-time lead.
- During the pre-launch phase, one person may cover multiple roles; coverage must be documented.

## KPIs
- Role coverage (every critical task has an owner and backup)
- SOP training completion rate
- Average escalation resolution time
- Employee/contractor turnover in critical roles

## Dependencies
- Organization_Structure.md
- Approval_Matrix.md
- Escalation_Matrix.md
- SOPs and playbooks

## Best Practices
- Use the RACI format for cross-functional processes.
- Keep decision authority clear to avoid delays during incidents.
- Revisit roles monthly in the first 90 days after launch.

## Review Frequency
Monthly until launch; quarterly after.
