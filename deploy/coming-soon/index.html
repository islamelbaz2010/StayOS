What is MAKAZOH?

A hospitality marketplace for Egypt — where travellers discover and book stays, and owners list and manage them.

When does it launch?

Final preparation is underway — a confirmed date is announced here and shared first with hosts who registered their interest.

Who can register as a host?

Owners and operators — apartments, villas, chalets, serviced apartments, hotels. Individuals and management companies welcome.

Are bookings open?

No — this page is for pre-launch registration only.

What do founding hosts get?

Priority application review, early onboarding invitation, and a product intro session — commercial terms are agreed during onboarding.
