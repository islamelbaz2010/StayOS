## Gate Rule

The benchmark closure register (`FINAL_AIRBNB_BENCHMARK_CLOSURE.md`) is the sole arbiter of gate status.

- **UNLOCKED (condition met for code)**: the register currently contains **0 unresolved IMPLEMENTABLE benchmark gaps** (LIST 2 is empty — both gaps found were implemented in the closure pass).
- **Remaining open items** are founder product decisions (LIST 3) and non-product release blockers (LIST 4). They do not re-gate differentiation work, but ideas that depend on them must record the dependency below.
- No differentiation idea may be implemented until the founder enters it in the framework below and signs the `FOUNDER DECISION` field.

## Notes for the Founder

- "CURRENT AIRBNB BEHAVIOR" should cite observable Airbnb product behavior, not assumptions — this keeps differentiation claims honest.
- Ideas touching payments, identity, personal data, or marketplace role automatically require the `LEGAL/REGULATORY DEPENDENCIES` field and a `LEGAL/ACCOUNTING REVIEW ITEM` entry in the technical handoff before implementation.
- Ideas that depend on unresolved LIST 3 decisions (e.g., guest-type split, alterations, special offers, processor choice) may be drafted now but should record that dependency explicitly.
