# StayOS — Brand & CI Brief

Status: working brief for the Brand/Marketing team to produce the final
visual identity. Values marked "current product" reflect the shipped UI;
creative direction items are **RECOMMENDED**.

---

## 1. Brand character

Hospitality-first, modern, uncluttered. StayOS should feel like a
well-run boutique stay brand with marketplace scale — trustworthy,
local, calm.

## 2. Tone of voice

- **English:** clear, warm, concise; short sentences; no jargon.
- **Arabic:** Modern Standard Arabic, conversational register; natural
  phrasing, not literal translation. Arabic is a first-class voice, not
  a translation of English.
- Never: hype ("revolutionary"), fee jargon, legalese, dark patterns.

## 3. Visual direction

**RECOMMENDED:** editorial hospitality — generous whitespace, strong
photography, restrained accent color. Think "premium travel magazine",
not "coupon site".

## 4. Color direction

Current product palette (keep consistent):

- **Accent:** lime/neon green (`brand` scale in product theme) — used
  with restraint for CTAs and highlights.
- **Type/ink:** near-black for headings and primary text.
- **Surfaces:** white / light neutrals.
- **Secondary accent:** warm amber family for highlights/banners.

**RECOMMENDED:** final CI should formalize exact hex/usage ratios
(~90% neutral, ~10% accent) and accessible contrast pairs for both
light surfaces and dark brand moments (e.g. dark CTA bands).

## 5. Typography direction

- **English/Latin:** a clean geometric or neo-grotesque sans for UI and
  headlines (current product uses a system sans stack).
- **Arabic:** a legible modern Arabic face with good diacritic rendering
  and matching weight range; must pair harmoniously at mixed-script
  sizes.
- **Hierarchy (current):** bold tight headlines, regular comfortable
  body, small muted metadata.

## 6. Photography

- Real Egyptian properties and destinations; natural light; lived-in but
  tidy interiors; human presence sparing and candid.
- Avoid stock-photo clichés (generic skylines, handshakes, staged smiles).
- Alt text is required on every CMS image (accessibility + Arabic
  localization).

## 7. Iconography

**RECOMMENDED:** thin-stroke, rounded, minimal icon set consistent with
the product's neutral UI; avoid filled/heavy icons or multi-color glyphs.

## 8. Motion

**RECOMMENDED:** subtle — fast fades/slides (150–250ms), no bounce, no
parallax gimmicks. Motion should never delay a booking action.

## 9. Logo requirements

Deliverables for the Brand team: primary wordmark, monogram/app icon,
light & dark variants, RTL-safe lockup, minimum sizes, clear-space
rules, favicon set.

## 10. Social identity requirements

- Avatar/monogram readable at 48px.
- Template families: destination card, quote/testimonial, announcement,
  campaign key visual — all bilingual layouts (RTL-safe).
- Keep accent color consistent with product lime.

## 11. Digital product relationship

Marketing surfaces (CMS pages, campaigns, social) inherit the product
palette and type scale. Campaign pages must not introduce off-brand
fonts/colors — CMS block rendering already enforces the product look.

## 12. DO / DON'T

**DO**
- Lead with real places and real hosts.
- Keep layouts airy; let photography carry emotion.
- Localize idiomatically — write Arabic-first where the audience is Arabic.

**DON'T**
- Don't expose or advertise internal fee structure (all-inclusive only).
- Don't use "escrow" or custody language in marketing copy.
- Don't overuse the lime accent — restraint is the brand.
- Don't mix in third-party OTA visual conventions (star bursts, slashes,
  countdown pressure tactics).
