FAQ

Questions, answered.

What is MAKAZOH?

MAKAZOH is a hospitality marketplace for Egypt and the region — a platform where travellers discover and book stays, and property owners list and manage them.

When will the platform launch?

We are in final preparation. A confirmed public launch date will be announced on this page and to registered hosts and guests — we don't publish dates before they're certain.

Which locations are targeted?

Egypt first — including Cairo, Alexandria, the North Coast, Ain Sokhna, Hurghada and the Red Sea — with regional expansion to follow.

Who can register as a Host?

Property owners and accommodation providers — apartments, villas, chalets, serviced apartments, hotels and resorts. Individuals and management companies are both welcome.

Is the platform accepting bookings today?

No — bookings are not yet open to the public. This page is for pre-launch registration of hosts and interested guests.

How will interested users be contacted?

By the contact method you choose at registration — phone, WhatsApp or email. We only contact you about MAKAZOH, and only with your consent.
