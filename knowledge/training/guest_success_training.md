## Purpose

This is the complete training program for Guest Success team members. By end of training, the team member can independently manage the full guest lifecycle, from pre-arrival through post-stay review collection.

---

## Training Overview

**Duration**: 4 days
**Format**: Read → Shadow → Practice → Assessment
**Assessment**: End-to-end guest journey roleplay assessment

---

## Day 1: Understanding the Guest

### Module 1.1: The Guest Journey

Read `knowledge/customer_success/guest_lifecycle.md` completely.

The guest has 7 stages. Your job changes at each stage:

1. **Discovery**: You are not involved (marketing/SEO handles discovery)
2. **Evaluation**: You may answer property questions via in-app or WhatsApp
3. **Booking**: You confirm and send booking confirmation proactively
4. **Pre-Arrival**: You ensure the guest has everything they need to arrive
5. **Stay**: You monitor and respond; welfare check at 30 minutes post-check-in
6. **Departure**: You coordinate checkout and collect review
7. **Return**: You proactively bring the guest back

**The three moments where guest success can make or break the relationship**:
- Pre-arrival (24h before check-in): Does the guest have clear, complete information?
- 30 minutes post-check-in: Is everything okay?
- 2 hours after checkout: Review request and experience follow-up

---

### Module 1.2: Egyptian vs. GCC Guest Profiles

Read the GCC-specific section in `knowledge/hospitality/guest_host_expectations.md`.

Key differences you must internalize:

| Dimension | Egyptian Guest | GCC Guest |
|-----------|---------------|-----------|
| Communication language | Egyptian Arabic (عامية) | Formal Modern Standard Arabic (فصحى) |
| Service expectations | Reasonable hospitality | High — comparable to 4-star hotel |
| Privacy importance | Moderate | Very high |
| Group size | 2–8 typically | 6–20 common (extended family) |
| Price sensitivity | High | Low (quality over price) |
| Documentation needs | Minimal | High (receipts, confirmations) |
| Ramadan behavior | Varies | Strong observance expected |

**Practical implication**: Write your WhatsApp messages to Egyptian guests in Egyptian colloquial Arabic (عامية). Write to GCC guests in Standard Arabic (فصحى). Getting this wrong is a signal of low professionalism that GCC guests notice immediately.

## Day 2: Pre-Stay and Check-In Excellence

### Module 2.1: Pre-Arrival Communication

**24 hours before check-in**: Send Template A2 from `knowledge/support/communication_templates.md`. This message confirms:
- Full address with building name and floor
- Check-in time
- Access code
- WiFi credentials

If any of this information is missing from the booking record, find it before sending — contact the host if necessary.

**What "unclear address" means** and why it matters: Cairo addressing is notoriously inconsistent. "Maadi, Block 1" is not an address. A guest arriving at 9pm in an unfamiliar neighborhood, with 3 children, to a vague address is a disaster waiting to happen. The confirmation message must include: building name, street address, floor number, apartment number, GPS coordinates link.

---

### Module 2.2: The 30-Minute Welfare Check

This is non-negotiable. 30 minutes after the guest's scheduled check-in time, send Template B1 (or your adapted version):

"أهلاً [Name]، وصلتم وكل حاجة تمام؟"

Do not skip this, even if you're busy. Even if you believe everything went smoothly. The 30-minute check:
- Catches access problems early (guest is still outside, code doesn't work)
- Catches property condition problems early (something looks different than photos)
- Signals to the guest that there is a human being who cares about their experience

A guest who checks in without ever hearing from StayOS is a guest who, when something goes wrong at 11pm, feels alone.

## Day 3: Mid-Stay and Checkout

### Module 3.1: Issue Response During Stay

Any issue reported during the stay goes through the support triage system. Your role in Guest Success:
- You receive the issue
- Classify severity (CRITICAL / HIGH / MEDIUM / LOW)
- If CRITICAL or HIGH: escalate to Operations immediately, then handle communication
- If MEDIUM or LOW: handle communication; coordinate resolution

The most important rule: **never leave a guest waiting more than 15 minutes for a response to a HIGH or CRITICAL issue.** A guest with a broken AC at 10pm who hasn't heard back in 30 minutes is about to post on social media.

---

### Module 3.2: Checkout and Review Collection

**Evening before checkout**: Send Template D1 (checkout reminder).

**2 hours after checkout**: Send Template D2 (review request).

**Why the review is critical**:
Read `knowledge/customer_success/guest_lifecycle.md` — the departure stage section.

Reviews are the marketplace's quality signal. A host with no reviews or bad reviews doesn't get bookings. A platform with low review rates doesn't look credible to new users. Review collection is not optional — it is part of the job.

**What improves review response rate**:
- Sending the request exactly at 2 hours post-checkout (not 5 days later)
- Personalizing the message (use the guest's name, reference their property name)
- Asking for feedback, not just a star rating: "رأيك هيفيدنا ويفيد الضيوف الجايين"
- Making the link one click (no login required to leave a review)

## Day 4: Assessment

### Roleplay Assessment: End-to-End Guest Journey

You play the Guest Success role for a fictional guest. The assessor plays the guest.

**Journey events** (you don't know what they are in advance):
- Guest asks a question about the property 24 hours before check-in
- Guest receives pre-arrival message from you
- Guest reports a minor issue at check-in (you handle it)
- Guest has a smooth stay and checkout
- Guest is asked for a review

**Pass criteria**:
- Pre-arrival message sent with all required information
- 30-minute welfare check completed (even though no issue reported)
- Issue response within SLA and resolved correctly
- Review request sent at correct timing with correct personalization
- All communication in appropriate Arabic register (Egyptian vs. formal depending on guest type)
