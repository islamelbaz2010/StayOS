## Purpose

This is the complete training program for anyone in a Host Success role at StayOS. By the end of this training, the team member can independently handle all standard host interactions, from first contact through performance coaching.

## Training Overview

**Duration**: 5 days (1 week)
**Format**: Read → Shadow → Practice → Supervised → Independent
**Assessment**: Pass/fail roleplay assessment on Day 5

## Day 1: Foundation Knowledge

### Module 1.1: Understand the Business Model

Before speaking to a single host, you must understand why hosting works economically:

Read these in order:
1. `knowledge/marketplace/marketplace_lifecycle.md` — How the marketplace works, what Stage 1 means
2. `knowledge/finance/escrow_model.md` — How host payment works (the escrow model)
3. `knowledge/finance/payout_operations.md` — The payout mechanics hosts ask about most

**Assessment questions you must be able to answer after Day 1**:
- If a host asks "when do I get paid?" — what is the exact answer? (24h after check-in + batch timing + bank processing = 2–4 business days)
- If a host asks "why does StayOS take commission?" — what is the value explanation? (Trust infrastructure, payment protection, guest acquisition, dispute resolution)
- If a host asks "what happens if a guest damages my property?" — what is the process? (Trust & Safety dispute, security deposit application, evidence requirement)

### Module 1.2: Know the Host Journey

Read `knowledge/customer_success/host_lifecycle.md` completely.

Key stages to internalize:
- **Prospect**: Host has heard about StayOS but hasn't signed up. Your job: make the value clear, address fears.
- **Onboarding**: Host has signed up and is completing KYC + listing setup. Your job: remove every obstacle within 48 hours.
- **Activation**: First booking confirmed. Critical 21-day window. Your job: ensure the first experience is excellent.
- **Growth**: Regular bookings, building the host-platform relationship. Your job: coaching and optimization.
- **Maturity**: Consistent high-performer. Your job: don't mess it up; support and recognize.
- **Advocacy**: Host actively refers others. Your job: make it easy and rewarding.

## Day 2: Host Onboarding Process

### Module 2.1: The Onboarding Checklist

Every host must complete, in order:
1. Account registration (email, phone, password)
2. KYC identity verification (national ID + selfie)
3. Listing creation (property details, photos, pricing, house rules)
4. Property inspection (conducted by StayOS field team)
5. Payout routing setup (bank account, verified legal name match)
6. Calendar setup (availability, minimum stay, advance notice)
7. Listing review and approval by StayOS

**Your role in each step**:
- Steps 1–2: Guide the host through the app; refer KYC issues to `knowledge/trust/identity_verification_guide.md`
- Steps 3–4: Assist with listing quality, photo guidance, pricing suggestion; coordinate inspection
- Steps 5–6: Verify legal name match against KYC (BR-ID-02); guide calendar setup
- Step 7: Quality check before approving; ensure all required fields are complete

---

### Module 2.2: Host Objection Handling

These are the most common objections and the correct responses:

**"I'm not comfortable with strangers in my property."**
"That's completely understandable — it's your home. Every guest is identity-verified before they can book. You'll see their name and verification status before any booking is confirmed. And our escrow model means they're financially accountable for the stay. Would you like me to walk you through exactly what the verification process looks like?"

**"What if something gets damaged?"**
"We require a security deposit on every booking, collected from the guest before check-in. If there's damage, you report it with before-and-after photos. Our Trust & Safety team investigates and charges the guest's deposit accordingly. The key is: pre-stay photos of the property document its condition, which makes any damage claim straightforward to prove."

**"How do I know I'll actually get paid?"**
"The guest's full payment is collected by StayOS before check-in. It's held in escrow. 24 hours after the guest checks in and doesn't file a major complaint, your payout is automatically initiated. You don't have to chase payment — it comes to you automatically."

**"The commission seems high."**
"I understand — 10% is a meaningful amount. What it buys you: identity-verified guests (not strangers off the street), payment security (you're guaranteed your payout after check-in), dispute resolution if something goes wrong, and guest acquisition (we bring you guests who wouldn't have found you otherwise). Would you like to calculate your estimated monthly earnings with our pricing tool?"

## Day 3: Property Quality and Listing Optimization

### Module 3.1: Property Quality Standards

Read `knowledge/hospitality/property_quality_standards.md` completely.

Know these cold:
- Essential / Comfort / Premium tier definitions and price ranges
- What a property inspection covers (structural, safety, cleanliness, photography)
- Cultural flags (Family-Only, Halal-Certified, Women-Only, GCC-Ready) and what they require

**Practical exercise**: Visit one StayOS-listed property with an experienced team member. Walk through the inspection checklist. Take the photos yourself. Review your photos against the standard.

---

### Module 3.2: Listing Photography Coaching

The single biggest driver of listing-to-booking conversion is photo quality. Poor photos → low views → no bookings → host churn.

**Photo requirements**:
- Minimum 10 photos per listing
- Living room (wide angle, daylight)
- Kitchen (showing appliances, clean surfaces)
- Bedroom(s) — each bedroom gets its own photo
- Bathroom(s) — each bathroom, clean, showing fixtures
- Balcony or outdoor space if exists
- Building entrance and elevator (safety context)
- Neighborhood landmark or street view

**What makes a good photo**:
- Natural daylight (not flash)
- Wide angle (phone camera + wide lens attachment)
- All corners of the room visible
- No personal items of the host (remove family photos, medications, personal documents)
- Fresh linens on all beds
- Clean and staged (items on counters put away)

**When host-provided photos are not adequate**:
- Tell the host clearly and specifically: "Your bedroom photo is too dark — can you take it in the morning when daylight is coming through the window?"
- Do not approve listings with poor photos. A listing with bad photos will not convert, which means the host won't get bookings, which means host churn. This hurts both of you.
- If the host cannot get better photos: offer to coordinate a professional photo visit (at StayOS cost for strategic supply).

---

### Module 3.3: Pricing Coaching

Read `knowledge/marketplace/marketplace_health_kpis.md` — the section on GMV per active listing.

**Pricing guidelines by tier**:
- Essential: EGP 300–800/night
- Comfort: EGP 800–2,000/night
- Premium: EGP 2,000–6,000/night

**For new hosts**: Recommend 5–10% below the comparable properties in their tier for the first 3 months. The goal is to build a review history quickly. Once they have 5+ positive reviews, they can price at or above market.

**Common pricing mistakes to correct**:
- Overpricing at launch (hoping to attract quality guests; actually attracts zero guests)
- Underpricing out of fear (signals low quality to guests; attracts price-sensitive guests only)
- Not adjusting for seasonality (Ramadan, summer, Eid periods have different demand profiles)

## Day 4: Retention and Performance Coaching

### Module 4.1: The 21-Day Rule

Read the "days to first booking" section of `knowledge/customer_success/host_lifecycle.md`.

**The most important number you will manage**: 21 days. The time between a host going live and their first booking. Above 21 days: active intervention required. Above 30 days: 95% churn probability.

**What to do on Day 22 with no booking**:
1. Call the host (not WhatsApp — a phone call shows you care)
2. Diagnose: calendar issues? pricing? listing quality? 
3. Take specific action in the call: adjust pricing, unlock calendar, refresh listing description
4. Follow up in 7 days

---

### Module 4.2: Ongoing Performance Coaching

For active hosts (at least monthly):

**Metrics to review with the host**:
- Number of views on their listing this month vs. last month
- View-to-inquiry rate (if low: listing quality issue)
- Inquiry-to-booking rate (if low: host response time or pricing issue)
- Guest review scores (if below 4.3: quality coaching needed)

**How to have a performance coaching conversation**:
Opening: "أهلاً [Name]! بعمل مراجعة شهرية لأرقام وحدتك. عايز نتكلم عن نقطتين هيساعدوا في تحسين الحجوزات."

The host is not a student receiving feedback. They are a partner being coached. Frame every observation as "what we can do together to improve" not "what you're doing wrong."

## Day 5: Assessment

### Roleplay Assessment (Pass/Fail)

**Scenario 1**: A host who has been live for 28 days with no bookings. Their pricing is 15% above comparable listings, and their kitchen photo is dark. Conduct the call.

**Scenario 2**: A host calls upset because their payout is lower than they expected. They paid EGP 3,000 to list but only received EGP 2,700. Explain clearly.

**Scenario 3**: A host says they want to collect cash from guests directly and not use the platform for payment. How do you respond?

**Pass criteria**:
- Arabic language quality: fluent, natural (not translated)
- Issue diagnosed correctly within 2 minutes
- Specific action taken or explained (not "we'll look into it")
- Tone: warm and professional, not defensive
