## Purpose

This is the complete training program for Support team members at StayOS. By end of training, the team member can independently handle all support contacts within SLA, classify severity correctly, and escalate appropriately.

---

## Training Overview

**Duration**: 4 days
**Format**: Read → Shadow 20 live contacts → Supervised handling of 10 contacts → Independent
**Assessment**: SLA-timed response exercise, written Arabic assessment

---

## Day 1: Foundation

### Module 1.1: The Support Mission

StayOS support is not a cost center. It is a trust mechanism. Every support interaction is an opportunity to prove that StayOS is a real company that stands behind its guests and hosts. A guest who has a problem and gets it resolved in 10 minutes is more loyal than a guest who never had a problem.

Read these before anything else:
1. `knowledge/support/support_workflows.md` — The full support process
2. `knowledge/support/escalation_playbook.md` — When and how to escalate

**The four pillars** (memorize these):
1. Speed: First response within SLA every time
2. Clarity: One message that fully answers the question
3. Authority: You can resolve standard issues without manager approval
4. Follow-through: Issue is not closed until the customer confirms it's resolved

---

### Module 1.2: Your Authority

Support agents at StayOS have more authority than at most companies. You are authorized to:
- Issue goodwill credits up to EGP 300 per booking — without asking anyone
- Extend check-in flexibility up to 2 hours — without asking anyone
- Confirm cancellation and refund timelines — without asking anyone
- Resend any booking documentation — without asking anyone

This authority exists so you can resolve issues in one interaction. Use it. Don't say "let me check with my manager" for issues within your authority — that is a failure of the support interaction.

---

### Module 1.3: SLA Memorization

Write these down. Know them without looking:

| Severity | First Response | Resolution |
|----------|---------------|-----------|
| CRITICAL | 5 minutes | 2 hours |
| HIGH | 15 minutes | 4 hours |
| MEDIUM | 1 hour | 24 hours |
| LOW | 4 hours | 24 hours |

**How to classify**: See the classification table in `knowledge/support/support_workflows.md`.

## Day 2: Arabic Communication Standard

### Module 2.1: Language Requirements

All support at StayOS is Arabic-first. This means:
- You write the Arabic message first
- English follows below, if the contact has been communicating in English
- Your Arabic must be natural Egyptian Arabic for standard interactions (عامية) and formal Arabic for formal or complaint contexts

**What "natural Arabic" means**: If you translated your message from English to Arabic in your head before writing it, the Arabic will not sound natural. Write in Arabic directly, as you would speak it.

**Written Arabic exercise** (to complete before Day 2 assessment):
Write responses to these situations in Arabic, without looking at templates:
1. A guest asks what time they can check in
2. A host asks where their payout is
3. A guest reports their AC isn't working

Your responses will be evaluated for natural language quality. Google Translate quality is a fail.

---

### Module 2.2: Communication Templates

Read `knowledge/support/communication_templates.md` completely.

**How to use templates**:
1. Templates are starting points, not scripts
2. Always fill in [placeholders] before sending — every one of them
3. If the situation doesn't exactly match a template, adapt the closest template
4. If the situation has no template: handle it, then propose a new template to the Support Lead

**Practice exercise**: For each template in Category A and B, write a personalized version for a specific fictional booking (make up the names, amounts, and dates). This ensures you understand how to adapt, not just copy.

## Day 3: Scenario Practice

### Shadow Exercise

On Days 2–3, you shadow an experienced support agent handling live contacts. Your role:
- Read each incoming message
- Write your response privately (don't send it)
- Compare your response to what the agent sends
- Discuss any differences

Minimum 20 contacts before proceeding to supervised handling.

### Common Scenarios to Master

**Scenario 1: The Access Code That Doesn't Work**
Guest: "الرمز مش شغال وأنا واقف قدام الباب"

1. Verify the correct code from the booking record immediately
2. Send the correct code clearly with usage instructions
3. Call the guest if no confirmation within 5 minutes
4. If still blocked → escalate to Operations (Level 3) and start the lockout playbook

**Scenario 2: The Billing Complaint**
Host: "حسبتوا عليا عمولة غلط"

1. Pull the booking immediately
2. Calculate the commission clearly (gross × commission rate)
3. Show the breakdown in the message
4. If the amount is genuinely wrong: escalate to Finance immediately

**Scenario 3: The Post-Stay Refund Request**
Guest (2 days after checkout): "الشقة كانت وسخة وعايز استرداد"

1. Ask: "When did you report the cleanliness issue during your stay?"
2. Pull the support contact history during the stay
3. If there was no in-stay report: "Disputes must be reported during the stay to allow us to resolve them. We're sorry you had this experience — we'd like to understand more to prevent it for future guests. As a goodwill gesture, we can apply a [amount] credit to your next booking."
4. If there WAS an in-stay report: escalate to Trust & Safety for dispute investigation

**Scenario 4: The Angry Guest**
Guest: "أنا بقالي ساعتين بحاول أتواصل معاكم ومحدش بيرد" (been trying to reach you for 2 hours, nobody answers)

First: Do NOT respond to this type of message with an apology first. Respond with action first:
"أهلاً [Name]! أنا [Name] من فريق StayOS هنا معاك دلوقتي. محتاج أساعدك في إيه؟"

Then resolve. Then acknowledge the wait at the end, not the beginning.

## Day 4: Assessment

### Assessment 1: SLA Speed Test

You are given 5 support scenarios, one at each severity level (CRITICAL through LOW). You have a clock. You must:
- Classify the severity correctly
- Write and send (into the test system) the correct first response within the SLA for that severity
- The response must be in Arabic first

**Pass criteria**: 4 of 5 responses within SLA and correctly classified.

### Assessment 2: Escalation Judgment

You are given 5 scenarios. For each, answer: (a) should you resolve this or escalate? (b) if escalate, to which level? (c) what information do you include in the escalation?

**Pass criteria**: 4 of 5 correct escalation decisions.

### Assessment 3: Written Arabic Quality

You are given 3 situations. Write natural Arabic responses, no templates. Graded by the Support Lead on natural language quality.

**Pass criteria**: All 3 rated ≥4/5 on natural language quality.
