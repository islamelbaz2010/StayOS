# EARLY DEMAND PLAYBOOK — StayOS

**Prepared by:** Executive Product Director, Growth Director, Operations Director  
**Date:** 2026-08-03  
**Purpose:** Define how StayOS acquires its first 10, 100, and 500 guests during Stage 1.

---

## 1. Demand Acquisition Philosophy

Supply is the scarce side in Stage 1, but demand must be deliberately manufactured. A marketplace with 50 listings and zero guests is still not a marketplace. The first guests are not acquired through advertising. They are acquired through founder network, trust, and manual matching.

**Evidence from the repository:**
- `knowledge/marketplace/cold_start_playbook.md` — first 10 transactions, manual operations

## 2. Customer Personas

### 2.1 Persona A: Egyptian Domestic Traveler

**Profile:**
- Age 25–45
- Middle to upper-middle income
- Traveling with family or spouse
- Books 1–3 trips per year inside Egypt
- Uses WhatsApp, Facebook, Instagram
- Price-sensitive but values cleanliness, AC, and location

**Triggers:**
- Weekend in Cairo
- Eid or summer vacation
- Medical or business trip
- Family visit

**Objections:**
- "Is this real?"
- "Can I pay with Fawry/InstaPay?"
- "What if the property is not as described?"

### 2.2 Persona B: GCC Tourist

**Profile:**
- Saudi, UAE, Kuwait national
- Family of 4–6
- Planning Egypt holiday
- Used to Booking.com/Airbnb but frustrated with English-first UX
- Values family privacy, halal-friendly, Arabic support

**Triggers:**
- Summer holiday in Cairo/Alexandria
- Ramadan or Eid travel
- Medical tourism
- University visit

**Objections:**
- "Do they accept Mada/Apple Pay?"
- "Is the neighborhood safe for families?"
- "Will someone answer in Arabic if there is a problem?"

### 2.3 Persona C: Corporate Travel Booker

**Profile:**
- HR or facilities manager
- Books apartments for visiting staff
- Needs receipts and corporate billing
- Books 10–50 room-nights per month

**Triggers:**
- Short-term project in Cairo
- Executive visit
- Training or conference

**Objections:**
- "Can I get an invoice?"
- "Can we pay by bank transfer?"
- "Can I book multiple units?"

### 2.4 Persona D: Medical Tourist

**Profile:**
- Patient + family traveling for medical care
- Stays 3–14 days
- Needs proximity to hospital
- Values cleanliness, quiet, kitchen access

**Triggers:**
- Surgery or treatment in Cairo
- Follow-up appointment

**Objections:**
- "Is the property near [hospital]?"
- "Can I book for a long stay?"
- "Is it clean and quiet?"

## 3. Acquisition Channels

### 3.1 Founder-Led Sales

**Target:** First 10 guests.

**Method:**
- Founder calls 50 personal contacts who travel to or within Egypt.
- Offer a discounted first stay in exchange for honest review.
- Handle booking manually via WhatsApp.

**Cost:** EGP 0 (founder time).
**Expected conversion:** 10–20%.
**Timeline:** Week 1.

### 3.2 Facebook Groups

**Target:** First 100 guests.

**Method:**
- Identify 20–30 active Facebook groups for Egyptian travel, expats, New Cairo residents, GCC travelers.
- Post once per week with a real listing, price, and trust messaging.
- Respond immediately to comments and DMs.
- Avoid spam; focus on value.

**Cost:** EGP 0.
**Expected conversion:** 1–3% of engaged members.
**Timeline:** Weeks 1–4.

### 3.3 WhatsApp

**Target:** First 10–50 guests.

**Method:**
- Create a WhatsApp Business account.
- Share listings directly with target guests.
- Use WhatsApp status for featured properties.
- Build a broadcast list of interested travelers.

**Cost:** EGP 0.
**Expected conversion:** 10–20% of engaged contacts.
**Timeline:** From Week 1.

### 3.4 Corporate Travel

**Target:** First 10–20 corporate guests.

**Method:**
- Founder reaches to 10 companies with Cairo operations.
- Offer corporate rate and direct billing.
- Provide invoice and tax documents.
- Guarantee support during stay.

**Cost:** EGP 0 (founder time).
**Expected conversion:** 20–30% of contacted companies.
**Timeline:** Week 2–4.

### 3.5 Medical Tourism

**Target:** First 10–20 long-stay guests.

**Method:**
- Partner with 2–3 medical facilitators or hospitals.
- Offer long-stay discounts (7+ nights).
- List properties near hospitals.
- Provide airport pickup add-on.

**Cost:** EGP 0–5,000 (partnership fees).
**Expected conversion:** 10–20% of referred patients.
**Timeline:** Week 3–6.

### 3.6 Travel Agencies

**Target:** First 20–50 guests.

**Method:**
- Partner with 3–5 local travel agencies.
- Offer 5% reseller commission.
- Provide agency login or manual booking form.
- Bundle StayOS stays with tours.

**Cost:** 5% commission.
**Expected conversion:** 1–2 bookings per agency per week.
**Timeline:** Week 4–8.

### 3.7 Referral Loops

**Target:** Scale from 100 to 500 guests.

**Guest referral program:**
- Refer a friend who completes a stay → EGP 250 credit.
- Referred guest gets 10% first-booking discount.

**Host referral program:**
- Host refers a guest who books → EGP 250 credit.

**Cost:** Credits, not cash.
**Expected conversion:** 10–15% of guests refer at least one person.
**Timeline:** Month 2+.

### 3.8 Arabic Social Media

**Target:** Scale to 500 guests.

**Method:**
- Instagram and TikTok content in Arabic.
- Before/after property videos.
- Guest testimonial reels.
- Trust artifact content ("We resolved a problem in 2 hours").

**Cost:** EGP 5,000–15,000/month for content and ads.
**Expected conversion:** 1–3% of engaged audience.
**Timeline:** Month 2+.

### 3.9 Search Engine Optimization (SEO)

**Target:** Long-term organic demand.

**Method:**
- Arabic landing pages for "شقة مفروشة في التجمع" and similar.
- Blog content: neighborhood guides, travel tips.
- Google Business Profile.

**Cost:** EGP 0–5,000/month.
**Expected conversion:** Low in Stage 1; long-term channel.
**Timeline:** Month 3+.

## 4. First Booking Strategy

### 4.1 First 10 Bookings

**Method:** Manual founder-led sales.

1. Founder identifies 50 warm contacts.
2. Offers discounted stays in target area.
3. Manually matches to best listings.
4. Collects payment via Paymob/Stripe or manual escrow.
5. Handles check-in personally or via Operations Agent.
6. Collects review within 24h of checkout.

**Incentives:**
- 15–25% discount for first booking.
- Free cancellation up to 48h before check-in.
- Welcome gift (water, snacks, local guide).

### 4.2 First 100 Bookings

**Method:** Founder network + Facebook groups + WhatsApp + corporate.

1. Launch "Founding Guest" campaign.
2. Invite 200 people to book at founding-guest rates.
3. Target 50% occupancy in first 30 listings.
4. Use WhatsApp broadcasts and Facebook posts.
5. Begin corporate outreach.

**Incentives:**
- Founding guest badge.
- 10% off first 3 bookings.
- Referral credit.

### 4.3 First 500 Bookings

**Method:** Add travel agencies, medical tourism, referral loops, and limited paid social.

1. Activate 5–10 travel agency partners.
2. Launch guest referral program.
3. Run Arabic Instagram/TikTok ads with trust content.
4. Expand to second neighborhood once first reaches liquidity.

**Incentives:**
- Loyalty program.
- Weekend packages.
- Corporate rates.

## 6. Demand Milestones

### 6.1 Liquidity Thresholds

- 20 listings: test with founder network only.
- 50 listings: begin limited organic demand generation.
- 100 listings: begin paid acquisition and referral loops.
- 250 listings: scale paid acquisition and corporate.
