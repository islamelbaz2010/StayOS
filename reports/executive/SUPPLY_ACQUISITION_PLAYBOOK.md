# SUPPLY ACQUISITION PLAYBOOK — StayOS

**Prepared by:** Executive Marketplace Operations Board  
**Date:** 2026-08-03  
**Purpose:** Define how StayOS acquires the first 50, 100, 250, and 500 units across every supply channel.

---

## 1. Supply Acquisition Philosophy

StayOS is a two-sided accommodation marketplace in Stage 1 (Ignition). The cold-start problem is the single greatest threat. Supply must come first, it must be concentrated, and it must be verified.

**Evidence from the repository:**
- `knowledge/marketplace/cold_start_playbook.md` — institutional supply first, manual operations, geographic concentration
- `knowledge/marketplace/marketplace_lifecycle.md` — Stage 1 rules
- `knowledge/founder/scaling_playbook.md` — Phase 1: institutional, semi-professional, individual hosts in sequence
- `MARKETPLACE_SUPPLY_STRATEGY.md` — cold start, claim listings, seed inventory, scaling milestones

**Operating principle:** Acquire institutional and B2B supply first. Individual host acquisition is slower and higher variance. Use it to augment, not lead.

## 2. Geographic Focus

**Closed Alpha area:** One or two adjacent Cairo neighborhoods.

**Recommended options (per `knowledge/founder/scaling_playbook.md`):**
- New Cairo + Rehab (new development, family demographics, airport proximity)
- Maadi + Degla (expat/professional demand)
- Zamalek + Garden City (historic, GCC tourism adjacency)

**Rule:** No expansion until the first area reaches 50 verified listings and 15+ bookable options for the majority of searches.

## 3. Supply Channels

### 3.1 Individual Hosts

**Definition:** Property owners or authorized lessors of 1–3 units.

**Acquisition method:**
1. Founder network and warm introductions.
2. Facebook/WhatsApp real-estate groups.
3. OLX listing owners (manual outreach).
4. Instagram/TikTok organic content.
5. Referral program (host refers host).

**Onboarding flow:**
1. Host sees ad/post or receives referral.
2. Clicks to host landing page or sends WhatsApp.
3. Enters phone number and OTP.
4. Completes KYC (ID + selfie + ownership proof).
5. Creates listing with ops support.
6. Uploads photos or schedules StayOS photography.
7. Sets pricing and calendar.
8. Publishes after admin verification.

**Expected conversion:**
- First 50: 10–15% of qualified leads become published listings.
- Next 100: 20–25% (with testimonials and trust artifacts).
- Beyond 250: 25–30% (word of mouth and brand recognition).

**Operational effort:**
- High touch per host (5–10 calls/messages).
- 1 onboarding specialist can handle ~10 individual hosts per week.
- Photography and inspection are the main bottlenecks.

### 3.2 Property Managers

**Definition:** Operators managing 3–50 residential or vacation units.

**Acquisition method:**
1. Direct founder outreach to known property managers.
2. Real-estate broker network.
3. Golf clubs and expat community groups.
4. Existing Airbnb/Booking.com property managers.
5. Industry associations (Egyptian real-estate management groups).

**Onboarding flow:**
1. Initial call or meeting with decision-maker.
2. Present 3-month pilot agreement.
3. Zero commission for 6 months; dedicated account manager.
4. Collect portfolio list (CSV or spreadsheet).
5. Bulk import or admin creates listings.
6. Verify documentation for each unit.
7. Schedule photo sessions for the portfolio.
8. Publish portfolio.

**Expected conversion:**
- First 5 property manager meetings: 1–2 signed (20–40%).
- Signed property manager: 5–20 listings each.
- To reach 50 units: 3–4 property managers.
- To reach 100: 6–8 property managers.

**Operational effort:**
- Lower per-unit effort than individual hosts after initial contract.
- Requires dedicated account manager.
- Portfolio verification is front-loaded.

### 3.3 Brokers

**Definition:** Licensed real-estate brokers with access to empty or short-term rental properties.

**Acquisition method:**
1. Identify brokers in target neighborhoods.
2. Offer EGP 500–1,000 per signed listing or EGP 1,000–2,000 per completed booking.
3. Sign a simple referral agreement.
4. Provide broker with lead form.

**Onboarding flow:**
1. Broker identifies property and owner.
2. Submits lead with owner contact and basic details.
3. StayOS qualifies the owner and property.
4. Owner completes KYC and listing setup.
5. Broker receives commission after first completed stay.

**Expected conversion:**
- Active broker: 2–5 listings per month.
- To reach 50: 5–10 active brokers.
- To reach 100: 10–20 active brokers.

**Operational effort:**
- Requires broker onboarding and commission tracking.
- Broker quality varies; manual vetting required.
- Fraud risk: brokers may submit fake leads.

### 3.4 Serviced Apartments

**Definition:** Professional apartment operators offering furnished units with hotel-like services.

**Acquisition method:**
1. Founder-level B2B outreach to operators in New Cairo, Maadi, Zamalek.
2. Contact serviced apartment brands (e.g., The Place, Hapi Hotels, local operators).
3. Offer distribution channel for domestic and GCC travelers.
4. Zero commission pilot for 3–6 months.

**Onboarding flow:**
1. Business development meeting.
2. Sign distribution agreement.
3. Receive unit inventory and availability data.
4. Bulk import or API integration (manual CSV first).
5. Verify each unit's photos and amenities.
6. Publish under operator's host account.

**Expected conversion:**
- One serviced apartment deal: 10–50 listings.
- To reach 50: 1–2 deals.
- To reach 100: 2–4 deals.
- To reach 250: 5–8 deals.

**Operational effort:**
- High upfront BD effort.
- Low ongoing ops per unit.
- Requires account manager and standardized service-level agreement.

### 3.5 Small Hotels & Boutique Hotels

**Definition:** Hotels with 5–50 rooms, often not on major OTAs or poorly listed.

**Acquisition method:**
1. Founder outreach to boutique hotel owners.
2. Identify hotels with weak Arabic/OTA presence.
3. Offer zero-commission pilot and Arabic-first exposure.
4. Provide simple extranet for availability.

**Onboarding flow:**
1. Meeting with hotel owner/revenue manager.
2. Sign pilot agreement.
3. Collect room inventory, pricing, and availability.
4. Create listings per room type.
5. Verify photos and amenities.
6. Publish.

**Expected conversion:**
- One small hotel: 5–30 listings.
- To reach 50: 2–4 hotels.
- To reach 100: 4–8 hotels.
- To reach 250: 8–15 hotels.

**Operational effort:**
- Requires B2B sales skill.
- Operational handoff is straightforward if hotel has staff.
- Quality control is critical (professional photos, accurate amenities).

### 3.6 Corporate Housing

**Definition:** Companies with long-stay apartments for employees; residential buildings with empty units; relocation service providers.

**Acquisition method:**
1. Outreach to HR and facilities managers at multinationals.
2. Contact relocation companies serving expats.
3. Approach building management companies with empty units.
4. Offer 30+ day stays and corporate billing.

**Onboarding flow:**
1. Corporate agreement with pricing and billing terms.
2. Collect unit inventory and availability.
3. Bulk import listings.
4. Verify units.
5. Publish for corporate bookings.

**Expected conversion:**
- Corporate housing is a slower sales cycle (1–3 months).
- One corporate client: 5–20 units.
- Likely contributor in Stage 2, not Closed Alpha.

**Operational effort:**
- High BD effort, long sales cycle.
- Requires B2B contracting.
- Lower priority for first 50–100.

### 3.7 Tourism Companies & Travel Agencies

**Definition:** Companies packaging tours, transport, and accommodation for domestic and GCC travelers.

**Acquisition method:**
1. Partner with tour operators in target cities.
2. Offer reseller commission (e.g., 5% of booking value).
3. Bundle StayOS stays with tours.

**Onboarding flow:**
1. Partnership agreement.
2. Provide access to select inventory.
3. Tourism company books on behalf of guests.
4. Track reseller commission.

**Expected conversion:**
- Tourism companies can drive demand more than supply.
- Some operators control inventory directly (vacation rentals).

**Operational effort:**
- More demand-side than supply-side.
- Useful for filling first bookings.

## 5. Acquisition Method Details

### 5.1 Founder Network Protocol

1. Founder lists 50–100 personal contacts who own or manage property.
2. Prioritize by neighborhood (target area first).
3. Personal WhatsApp or phone call.
4. Offer first-mover benefits.
5. Record outcome in lead tracker.

### 5.2 Property Manager Outreach

**Script:**
> "StayOS is the Arabic-first accommodation marketplace for Egyptian domestic and GCC travelers. We are not asking you to leave Booking.com. We are an additional channel with zero commission for 6 months, dedicated account management, and faster payout. We need a 3-month pilot. You keep all your other channels."

### 5.3 Broker Program

- Sign simple referral agreement.
- Provide broker with lead submission form.
- Pay after first completed stay.
- Cap monthly payout per broker.
- Blacklist brokers with fake leads.

### 5.4 Serviced Apartment Pitch

- Lead with incremental demand (domestic + GCC).
- Offer co-marketing.
- Guarantee response time.
- Provide occupancy dashboard.

### 5.5 Hotel Pitch

- Target boutique hotels not fully booked on weekends.
- Offer Arabic-first listing.
- Zero commission pilot.
- Direct WhatsApp support.

## 7. Risks and Mitigations

| Risk | Mitigation |
|------|------------|
| Institutional partners refuse | Offer zero-commission pilot, founder-led BD, 3-month commitment only. |
| Individual host churn | Fast first booking, pricing coaching, proactive host success calls. |
| Broker fraud | Pay only after first completed stay; manual lead verification. |
| Photo bottleneck | Hire/train 2 field photographers in target area. |
| Geographic spread | Reject supply outside target area; waitlist only. |
| Quality variance | Three-gate verification; reject substandard listings. |
